<H2>Registeration from Pakistan</H2>

<Paragraph>This part worries most people, so here are the exact steps I followed after my direct card payment kept failing.</Paragraph>


<NumberList>
  <li>Start your registration. Log in, pick your certification, and begin the registration process</li>
  <li>Attempt the normal payment first. but if the card or gateway fails (as it did for me), don't keep retrying. Switch to the voucher route instead</li>
  <li>Buy an exam voucher from <Link url="https://salesforcestore.pearsonvue.com/" linkText="Pearson VUE"/> store</li>
  <li>Wait for the voucher email. Within 24 hours I received an email containing my voucher number.<Figure src={Voucher} caption="Voucher email from Pearson VUE" /></li>
  <li>Return to the registration flow. and choose the voucher option at checkout</li>
  <li>Enter your voucher number in the <Highlight>Voucher/Promo Code</Highlight> field and click Apply. Once the voucher is applied successfully, the total amount should change to <Highlight>$0</Highlight></li>
  <li>Choose your exam date, time, and test center. (or the online-proctored option)</li>
  <li>Confirm and submit. You'll receive a confirmation email with your appointment details</li>
</NumberList>

<Paragraph>That single workaround removed all the friction. If you hit the same card or gateway issue from Pakistan, buying the voucher first is a simple fix.</Paragraph>

<H2>Exam Day</H2>
<Paragraph>I sat my exam at <Highlight>Software World International Center in Karachi</Highlight>, and honestly, the center itself was the least stressful part of the whole journey.</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>I arrived 30 minutes early, as recommended. The staff set up the computer for me, and I started right at my scheduled time. The setup was simple: a monitor, a mouse, and a keyboard. No accessories were allowed into the room. I was wearing a wristwatch, and both it and my phone had to go into a locker. I also brought CNIC, because it is needed for identity verification.</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>I finished within the allotted time and submitted. If you're nervous about the environment, don't be, it's quiet and focused, and the staff handle the logistics so you can concentrate.</Paragraph>

<H2>Results and Certification</H2>

<Paragraph>In my experience, my result appeared immediately after I submitted the exam, and the follow-up certification email arrived within 24 hours.</Paragraph>
<Paragraph>I want to be clear that this was my timeline, not a guarantee. Official guidance says to allow up to 24 hours for results and dashboard updates, so plan for that and don't panic if your badge takes a little time to show up.</Paragraph>

<H1>Final Thoughts</H1>
